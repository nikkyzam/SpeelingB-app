# src/components/learning — module notes

## Purpose and public interface
The screens a child works through for one word group (learn, spell, quiz, test), plus a free daily word. Components own the per-word bookkeeping (LearningFlow, WordMastery, ReviewSchedule, stars, buddy snack, badges); pages pick the words and own set-level finalisation. Everything except WordOfTheDay takes the child's words via `words` and never falls back to the bank (empty → a "No words to practise" message).
- `LearnMode` `{ words, onComplete?, onMoveToPractice?, onMoreWords?, difficulty? }` — a light word card: the word, its chunks, 🔊 Hear it / 🐢 Slowly, tap-to-hear meaning and sentence; difficulty, category, facts, synonyms and `hint` sit under "More about this word". The first card is spoken. "Got it!" opens `QuickCheck` with the word and sentence covered (🙈, with a "👀 Peek" that counts as `helped`); the word only counts when `onPass` fires. A word already met — passed earlier in this lesson (Previous, then "Got it!") or already in `getWordsLearnedTotal()` (replays: "Learn Again", "More Words" on the last group) — passes with "You know “x”! ⭐" and writes nothing (no stars, snack, `recordLearned`, `ReviewSchedule.record` or `completeWord`); the finish line counts only new words. Starts at the first word not in `getWordsLearnedTotal()`. Crossing the daily goal plays `sfx.fanfare`, fires `show-confetti`, says "You did it!" and shows the prompt with today's real count ("Let's Spell!" → `onMoveToPractice`; "More Words" carries on through this set when the goal came mid-set, and only on its last word goes to `onMoreWords`, which the hub wires to the next group; without it it finishes the set). On the finish screen "Let's Spell!" is the big button. A ⭐/🔥 trail sits at the top (`WordTrail`)
- `QuickCheck` `{ word, variant?, onPass(helped), isLast?, streak? }` — tap-only reading check under the covered card. Kind = `build`/`choose`/`missing` by `variant % 3` (words under 4 letters always `build`; `missing` never hides the first or last letter). The `build` tray is re-mixed until it isn't already in the answer's order. Praise is `praise(streak + 1)` with a `Burst`; "Show me" spells the word letter by letter while the slots fill (then `onPass(true)`). Unlimited soft misses, never auto-reveals. Tiles stay 44px on phones. The pass timer is tracked. Records nothing itself.
- `SpellMode` `{ words, onComplete?(ids, { correct, stars }), onMoveToQuiz?, isQuiz?, difficulty?, resume = true, summary = true }` — the word is asked bee-style (`sayPrompt`: word, sentence, word); type or say it. One checked answer per word, compared with `sameSpelling` (case, accents and stray spaces set aside). A hit pays `addStars(1)`, cheers, bursts and auto-advances after 1.5s; a miss is a soft card ("Nearly! It's …", the word as letter tiles, `explain(word, attempt).tip`, the letters spoken) and waits for "Next Word". "🤔 Stuck?" replaces Reveal and Skip: it never counts as correct, writes nothing and advances through `advanceTimer`. There is no Previous (going back re-opened an answered word and paid it twice), no score and no accuracy %. `resume={false}` starts at word 1 instead of the first word not practised today; `summary={false}` hides its own finish card. `onComplete` receives every id in the set — misses, "Stuck?" words and words before the resume point too — so a page must never credit them: each hit is credited on the spot.
- `QuizMode` `{ type: 'spell' | 'vocab', words, onComplete?(score, wrong, ids), onNextFlow?, difficulty? }` — `getDailyGoal(type)` random words from `words`; one answer per question behind ref guards, one cancellable auto-advance (2s on a hit, 3.5s on a miss or Skip, which shows the answer — longer for a spelled answer via `spellOutPause`, with the next prompt spoken `{ queue: true }` behind it), one finish, and `onComplete` once per set: "Try Again" replays aren't reported (each report ran the hub's `completeVocabQuiz` and moved the child on another word group). Only a new `type`/`words` resets that. Spelling compares with `sameSpelling`. Vocab = 3 meanings, each with its own 🔊, the child's own words' meanings tried first as decoys. A miss says "Nearly! It's …"; no ❌ or wrong counter; one star per word per mount; a warm finish whose main button is the next step.
- `TestAll` `{ words, timePerWord = 30, onMoveToGames?, difficulty? }` — one-line intro with honest counts and a big Start. Untimed by default; "⏱️ Try it with a timer" shows a slowly filling ring (no red, paused during feedback). Each word gets a soft flash and sfx; time-up and "Stuck?" show the answer gently. `sameSpelling`. Results lead with ⭐ stars (one per correct word, once per mount) and 🌱 tappable words to practise. Writes nothing to the learning ledgers. QuizMode and TestAll show "🧠 Practise the ones I missed" only after a miss: it navigates to `/review` with `state.missed` (the ids just missed; tricky needs two misses and a missed word isn't due until tomorrow, so `/review?mode=tricky` couldn't hold them).
- `WordOfTheDay` (no props) — one easy word per UTC date, same for every child; meaning and sentence are tap-to-hear; "Keep this word" works once per day, then "A new word tomorrow 🌅".
- `WordTrail` `{ total, current, solved?, mascot?, streak?, stars? }` — one stepping stone per word, a ⭐ when solved, the world mascot on the current word, a ⭐ stars chip and a 🔥 streak chip that pops at 3/5/10, and "Halfway there!"/"One to go!" (`trailCheer`); above 20 words a single track. Used by LearnMode, SpellMode, QuizMode and TestAll in place of "Word x/y" and bars
- `difficulty` never selects words in any component (the page does); it is an effect dependency, and TestAll displays it.

## Key files
- `LearnMode/index.tsx` — card, QuickCheck hand-off, stars/combo, goal prompt, joke-of-the-day finish screen. The phonetic line shows only a real pronunciation: the bank stores `/word/` (slashes included) for all but three words, which rendered "//tag//"
- `QuickCheck/index.tsx` — the three tap checks; uses `shuffle`/`misspell` from `src/components/games/shared/wordTricks.ts`
- `SpellMode/index.tsx` — typed/spoken spelling, one-answer guard, cancellable `advanceTimer`, `explain` tip. Accuracy on the finish screen is `tally.correct / tally.answered` for the round (it used to divide the streak-boosted score, showing 300%)
- `QuizMode/index.tsx` — spell/vocab quiz; `scoreRef`/`wrongRef` so totals reported from a timeout are current
- `TestAll/index.tsx` — timed self-test, local results only
- `WordOfTheDay/index.tsx` — date-hash pick from `wordBank.getWordsByDifficulty(1)`, `word_of_day_collected` flag

## How it talks to other modules
- `src/pages/LearningHub` (`/learn`) mounts Learn/Spell/Quiz/Test with memoised `groupWords` (slice of the level or `?seasonal=` list, size = learn goal). It passes no LearnMode `onComplete` on purpose, so a finished set lands on LearnMode's own celebration. SpellMode `onComplete` credits nothing (SpellMode credited each hit), calls `unlockGames()` once the group is spelled and words its Celebration from the ledger ("spelled 2 of your 5 words" until all are). QuizMode is reached only via SpellMode "Take Quiz" (shown once `isDailyGoalReached()`): spell → `onNextFlow` → vocab → `/games`; its `onComplete` credits nothing for a spelling quiz and runs `completeVocabQuiz` once per set of `groupIds` per visit; during a `?seasonal=` event it puts the saved main-list group back afterwards, as `goToGroup` never moves it. "Next up" in an event whose words are all met and spelled (and with no earlier group left to meet) is "Back to my words" → `/learn`, never "Learn these words again".
- `src/pages/Review` (`/review`, `?mode=tricky`, or `state.missed` from a quiz or test, which comes first) mounts SpellMode over those ids or ReviewSchedule due/tricky ids (fallback `getReviewWordIds`) with `resume={false} summary={false}`, and says "🔓 Finish this to open every game!" when the review is the last lock. Its stars are SpellMode's one per word plus a flat 3 for finishing (it used to pay `max(3, n)` on top, paying each word twice); `onComplete` → stars, and `markReviewDone()` only for the scheduled review (`due`/`sample` sources), which satisfies the review half of the games gate. `missed` and `tricky` sessions are extra practice: they never mark the review done or show the "🔓" banner (one just-missed word used to clear the day's review while due words waited); their answers still move ReviewSchedule, so clearing every due word opens the gate by itself; when that opens every game, its celebration says "Play Games!" and goes to `/games`. A review that is due with nothing it can show (saved ids the bank no longer knows) counts as done, so the gate can't lock for good. `src/pages/Home` mounts WordOfTheDay.
- `src/pages/DailyQuiz` (`/daily-quiz`, `/weekly-quiz`) uses none of these: its own loop, misses requeue until solved, `passDailyQuiz()` is the quiz half of the games gate. TestAll is practice-only and gates nothing; QuickCheck lives only inside LearnMode.
- Contexts: `useAudio().speak` in all; `useProgress().learningFlow` in all but QuickCheck/TestAll; `useTheme().world.mascot` in SpellMode.
- Writes per event:
  - LearnMode pass (first meeting only): `addStars(helped ? 1 : 2)`, `WordMastery.recordLearned()`, `ReviewSchedule.record(id, !helped)`, `BuddyService.earnSnack()`, `completeWord(id)`, `AchievementsService.recordDailyGoalComplete()` on the crossing word, `recordComboAchieved` when unaided.
  - SpellMode checked answer: `ReviewSchedule.record(id, correct)`. On a hit also `completeSpellQuiz([id], 0)`, `WordMastery.recordCorrect(id)` + `recordSpelled()`, `earnSnack`, `recordWordSpelled`, `recordComboAchieved`. Reveal and Skip write nothing.
  - QuizMode answer: `ReviewSchedule.record`, `recordWordSpelled(correct, true)`; spell-type hit → `completeSpellQuiz([id], 0)`. Vocab completion only via the page's `completeVocabQuiz` in `onComplete`: it bumps `selectedGroup`, so it runs once per set, never per answer.
  - WordOfTheDay keep: `completeWord`, `recordLearned`, `ReviewSchedule.record(id, false)` (met, not tested → box 1, due tomorrow), `earnSnack`, `addStars(3)`.
- Stars only via `useRewardStore().addStars` (→ PointsService, which makes FunFx fly stars to the header and the buddy bounce): SpellMode 1 per hit, QuizMode and TestAll 1 per right word per mount, LearnMode 1–2 per word, WordOfTheDay 3. There are no pretend points or percentages any more. A tone guard in `FunLearning.test.tsx` bans "Incorrect", "Wrong:" and ❌ in this folder, DailyQuiz, Review and LearningHub.
- Events: no component dispatches or listens directly; the services above emit and sync (see root and persistence CLAUDE.md). LearningHub listens to `MASTERY_EVENT` to refresh its challenge card.

## Conventions and gotchas
- What actually opens what: the hub's steps read `wordsLearnedTotal`/`wordsSpelledTotal` for the group. The games gate (`isGameUnlocked`, see progress CLAUDE.md) ignores the `gamesUnlocked`, `unlockGames()` and `vocabQuizUnlocked` writes made here. This module reaches it only through `completeWord` (today's learn goal for FREE_GAMES, and the daily-quiz pool, which includes a kept daily word) and through Review's SpellMode.
- `words` must be referentially stable (`useMemo` in the page). A new array re-runs the load effect: LearnMode/SpellMode jump back to the first unlearned/unpracticed word, and QuizMode reshuffles and zeroes the score. `learningFlow` is also a dependency; ProgressProvider keeps it stable via `useState`.
- SpellMode's start index uses `getWordsPracticedToday()`, which only `completeWord` fills.
- One answer per word: SpellMode's `isCorrect !== null` and QuizMode's `isAnswered` guards. The mic calls `handleSubmit` directly, so the guard stays inside it. Any auto-advance must go through SpellMode's single cancellable `advanceTimer`, or a "Next" tap plus the timeout skips a word.
- Hints belong only in LearnMode because it teaches rather than tests. `NoSpellingHelp.test.ts` exempts paths containing `LearnMode` and bans `show first letter|Get Hint|Hint Used|revealFirst` everywhere else, including `QuickCheck/`. Never add hint buttons to SpellMode, QuizMode or TestAll.
- Word sourcing: `wordScoping.test.ts` fails any `wordBank.getAllWords|getRandomWords|getWordsByDifficulty` here unless `.meaning` is within 90 chars. QuizMode's distractor line passes because it maps meanings, and a distractor is never spelled. WordOfTheDay is exempt by folder name. The `wordBank` imports in LearnMode, SpellMode and TestAll are unused; don't start calling them.
- `SpellingInput` takes ONE Enter handler (`onKeyPress` or `onKeyDown`). Its touch keypad's check key reaches the page only as a synthetic Enter on both events, so binding both submits twice and binding neither leaves the check key dead. A new typing file goes in `SPELLING_FILES`. QuickCheck is tap-only and not listed.
- Mic (SpellMode only): the button renders only when `useSpeechRecognition().supported`. There is no permission pre-check: a denial or error just ends `listening`, and typing is always available. `parseSpelling` joins tokens when every token is one character ("C. A. T" → cat) and otherwise takes the last token ("the word is cat" → cat). The parsed text is submitted at once as the word's one answer, after `mic.reset()` so it can't refire.
- Timers: QuizMode has no countdown on purpose. TestAll is the only timed screen, and its warning stays soft (`--text-soft`, no red "Hurry!").
- Masking: the sentence is masked on screen only, with `new RegExp(escapeForRegExp(word), 'gi')` everywhere (a first exact match left "Apparently, …" showing the answer); SpellMode speaks the whole sentence inside `sayPrompt`, so it no longer says "underscore".
- Spelled-out answers: after a miss the letters are read at rate 0.8, so QuizMode, TestAll and DailyQuiz keep the answer up for `spellOutPause(word, screenMinimum)` (`spellOutPause.ts`: ~0.55s a letter, never below the screen's old pause, capped at 10s) and speak the next prompt with `{ queue: true }` when the timer moves on. A tap on Next still cuts in.
- Checklist for changing a learning step:
  1. Pick the ledgers it moves: LearningFlow sets, WordMastery, ReviewSchedule, stars, snack, badges. Per-word writes stay in the component and set-level writes in the page callback.
  2. Trace the gates it affects: the hub ladder, FREE_GAMES, the daily-quiz pool and the review gate.
  3. Update the tests: `createMockLearningFlow` in the LearnMode/QuizMode tests for any new `learningFlow` call (a partial mock throws on unknown methods), `LearningHub.test.tsx` for ladder changes, `__tests__/unit/services/progress/LearningFlow*.test.ts` for service changes.

## Testing this module
- Component tests: `npx vitest run __tests__/unit/components/learning`. With the guards: `npx vitest run __tests__/unit/components/learning __tests__/unit/components/NoSpellingHelp.test.ts __tests__/unit/components/games/wordScoping.test.ts __tests__/unit/components/SpellingInput.test.tsx __tests__/unit/pages/LearningHub.test.tsx`
- `LearnMode.test.tsx` covers: the word counts only after QuickCheck (via "Show me"), Previous, `onComplete`, the goal prompt. It mocks `useProgress` with a partial `learningFlow`; the other services run for real on `setupTests.ts`'s in-memory localStorage. `Math.random` is fixed at 0.6.
- `SpellMode.test.tsx` covers: right/wrong feedback and the tip, SpellingInput attributes, Reveal, one answer per word, Next cancelling the auto-advance, accuracy as right/answered, and accents set aside. It uses the real ProgressProvider and LearningFlowController (FirebaseSync mocked) with fake timers.
- `LearningLedger.test.tsx` mounts the real hub, QuizMode, LearnMode, DailyQuiz and Review over a real LearningFlowController: one group move per finished vocab set (Try Again, a second lap, seasonal), only spelled words credited, replays pay nothing, the seasonal "Next up", spelled answers heard in full, masking, and which review sessions mark the review done.
- `QuizMode.test.tsx` renders inside a `MemoryRouter` (QuizMode and TestAll now use `useNavigate`) and covers vocab only: `Math.random` 0.5 makes the `sort(() => 0.5 - Math.random())` shuffles stable, and it checks the finish calls `onComplete(20, 0, ids)`.
- Guards: `NoSpellingHelp.test.ts` (SpellingInput usage, hint regex), `wordScoping.test.ts` (bank use), `SpellingInput.test.tsx` (keypad submits via keydown and keypress), `LearningHub.test.tsx` (hub ladder only; it mocks the LearningFlow module and never mounts a mode).
- Untested: QuickCheck's build/choose/missing success paths, WordOfTheDay, TestAll's behaviour, the mic path (jsdom has no SpeechRecognition, so the button never renders), `parseSpelling`, and most service side effects (LearningLedger covers spelled credit, group moves, review marking and LearnMode's replay writes).
