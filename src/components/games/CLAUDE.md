# src/components/games — module notes

## Purpose and public interface
Self-contained mini-games that give a child more looks at words they have already studied. Each game owns its rounds, scoring and feedback and reports one number back; GameCenter mounts one at a time and does all the paying out, so a game never navigates, keeps its own storage or awards stars itself (PrizeWheel and SiblingShowdown are the exceptions).
- Contract: `onComplete(score)` exactly once, when the last round settles; `words?: Word[]` (type from `src/services/wordBank`); an optional tuning prop with a default (`rounds`, `floors`, `dragonHp`, `duration`). GameCenter never passes the tuning prop, so the default is what ships
- `words` arrives shuffled, capped at 60, pre-filtered to a–z and 3+ letters, and only once 4+ exist (`MIN_GAME_WORDS`). It stays stable for the life of a mount
- Rescaling a game: its best is kept under `id@version` (`SCORE_SCALES` in `src/services/games/GameStats.ts`), so an old best on the old scale — or one an old device still writes — is never read. Bump the version whenever a game's score scale moves
- Payout in `GameCenter.handleGameComplete`: stars = `min(5, max(1, floor(score/100)))` (`MAX_GAME_STARS`, so a game scoring in the thousands can't pay 15), doubled for Game of the Day, plus best score (a new best plays `sfx.fanfare`), daily-challenge play and `AchievementsService.recordGamePlayed`. The shared `Celebration` plays `sfx.applause` when it opens. A clean run of a recent game scores roughly 150–400
- GameCenter honours `onComplete` once per opened game (a session in `finishFor`), and only while that game is still on screen; "Back to Games" ends the session. Several older games call `onComplete` from untracked timers or twice (an end-screen button plus its timer): that paid twice, paid for games left early, and let a stray timer close the next game a second after it opened. Keep tracking timers in new games anyway
- Named exports next to the default are fine for pure logic a test needs (`doubleAt` in DoubleTrouble)

## Key files
- `index.ts` — the barrel GameCenter imports from: every hub game, `PrizeWheel`, the two Bible games
- `types.ts`, `constants.ts` — dead (see root) but still re-exported by `index.ts`. `GameProps` has no `words` and `GAME_CONFIGS` doesn't match the catalogue, so don't type new games with them
- The fun kit — use it in every game rather than hand-rolling feedback:
  - `shared/useCheer.ts` — `cheer.hit({ say?, quiet? })` plays `sfx.correct` (plus `sfx.star` at 3/5/10… in a row), speaks a fresh line from `services/words/praise.ts` and returns it for the flash; `cheer.miss(...)` plays the soft `sfx.wrong` and a gentle "Nearly!"-type line; `cheer.streak()`. `quiet` for fast play, or when the next prompt would cut the praise off (BeeBingo)
  - `shared/useLater.ts` — `later(fn, ms)`, timers cleared on unmount. `shared/Burst` — `<Burst trigger={n} />` star firework inside a `position: relative` element (each game's `-flash` rule sets it), keyed on `trigger`, hidden with reduced motion. `shared/GameEmpty` — the no-button empty state
  - `shared/realWords.ts` — `isRealWord` (common words + `wordBank.getWordByWord`, a dictionary check only) and `fakeSpelling(word)`, a `misspell` that is never a real word. `shared/clues.ts` — `maskClue` (re-exported from `CrissCross/crossword.ts`)
  - `services/words/praise.ts` — `praise(streak)`, `nearly()`, `spellOut(word)` ("c, a, t": say the letters after a miss, not just the word), `sayPrompt(word, sentence)`
  - Speech: `AudioContext.speak` strips emoji (`speakable`), so a flash line can be spoken as-is; `speak(text, { queue: true })` waits instead of cutting off what's playing
- `shared/sfx.ts` — Web Audio synth `sfx.tap|correct|wrong|win|pop|star|whoosh|thud|applause|fanfare|worldJingle`. Honours the `audioEnabled` mute key; `setWorldSoundProfile` swaps the per-world tap and win tunes
- `shared/wordTricks.ts` — `shuffle`, `isPlayable(w, min)` (same a–z test as the hub), `misspell` (a believable typo; never doubles the first letter, since "ccat" gave the answer away), `splitChunk`, `rimeOf`/`rhymes`, `RHYME_FAMILIES`, `pickDistinct`, and the wrong-letter helpers `soundAlikes` (c/k/s, vowel for vowel), `likelySlips(word, i)` (the skipped half of a double, a false double, then sound-alikes; never random) and `letterTraps(word, i, n)` (slips topped up with random letters). Also used by `learning/QuickCheck`
- `BeeBingo` `makeCard(words, rand, isWord)` — deals the card and call order; a misspelling is dropped if `isRealWord` (from `shared/realWords`) says it's a word. Layouts are reshuffled until one line holds no misspelling, because four in the wrong places block all eight
- `CrissCross/crossword.ts` — `buildCrossword` (greedy, seeded by `rand`; drops words that can't cross onto rows of their own) and `maskClue` (blanks a word and its forms out of a definition; CrissCross runs every clue through it once per answer in the grid, since all clues are on screen together)
- `shared/plurals.ts` — `pluralOf` (letter rules), `pluralOfWord` (gated by `looksLikeNoun(meaning)`), `decoysFor`, `pickRounds`; used by WordMachine. Returning null means "leave the word out", never guess
- `WordChef/commonWords.ts` — `COMMON_WORD_SET` for checking a child's made-up words
- Catalogue (`*` = in GameCenter `wordlessGames`: no `words` prop, not gated on word count):
  - Type the whole word: WordScramble (tiles or typing, no clock; one more go, then shown for 0), SpellSprint (spoken, never printed: one blank per letter; races a bee that never ends the game), GhostWord (one peek, `lookTime` by length), MysteryPicture (two tries, then shown; the picture still uncovers), SpellTower (the roof goes on after the last word whatever happened; +100 only for a perfect tower), DragonDuel (at 0 hearts the dragon gets sleepy, so the duel always ends tamed), StoryBuilder (the star word is hidden while it's being spelled), SpellingAdventure (7 words over three places, no lives or clock), SpellTacToe (a move costs a word, vs the world's mascot), CrissCross (from the meaning, on its own keypad), WordLock (heard, then "have a go": up to 5 tries on its own keypad, each marked right/near/out by `markTry`)
  - Tap letters to spell: WordBuilder (a miss shows the letters, then half points), BalloonPop (blanks only; a wrong balloon wobbles and stays, so a word can always be finished), BeeCatch (the next slot is a glowing `_`, the letter shows after 2 misses), RescueTheBee (a bee in a jar that cracks and flies free), MissingLetter, VowelRescue, OnStage, SecretCode (keys show letters only; wrong letters clear, shown after 3), WordChef (every real word counts; goal ≤ 6 as blank chips), CaterpillarCrawl (steered to each letter past `letterTraps` decoys), HoneyFlight (a lane-runner: rows of three flowers, the next letter plus two `letterTraps`; a missed letter comes round again)
  - Spot the right or wrong spelling: SpellingCheck (prefix `sck-`; slips from `likelySlips`/`fakeSpelling`), TypoDetective (20/10/5 by try; "Read it to me"), WordWhack (every critter looks the same until tapped — the 🐛/🐝 face used to give it away; `onComplete` read through a ref so the hub's re-renders can't restart its clock), DoubleTrouble, TreasureTrail (a `moving` phase so a mashed die rolls once)
  - Word parts and sound: WordMachine (speaks the rule after a miss), WordFishing (`endingDecoys` always ends — ball/tall/call/fall used to freeze the tab), WordSort (`planSort`: at least two words in each basket), RhymeTime (`trueRhyme`: rhymes are matched by spelling, which only tracks sound in one-beat words, so the target and partner are one beat, neither is in `SOUNDS_ODD` — one, have, said, put… — and the ending isn't said two ways (`AMBIGUOUS_RIME`: d·own/gr·own, b·ead/h·ead); "favorite" used to pair with "kite" and "prone" with "one". `pickDecoys` bans the answer's whole family row: bee/sea, kite/white rhyme by ear), SyllableClap (prefix `scl-`; `isClappable`: only words where `countBeats` and `chunkWord` agree, minus the ones both get wrong together — a silent middle e after a long vowel like fo·re·cast or i·ce·berg, co/re/pre/de + vowel, and a short `UNSURE` list), WordJigsaw (drags `chunkWord` pieces past fakes; one-beat words split onset·rime), WordFamily* (hand-picked fakes filtered by `isRealWord`, a no-tricks bonus), HomophoneHero* (`dealPuzzles` shuffles the options — the answer used to always be first)
  - Recognise, order, remember: DefinitionDetective (clues through `maskClue`), MemoryMatch (6 pairs, cards read aloud on flip; was 1,200–1,500 points, now ~150–240), AbcOrder (an A–Z strip lights the first letters), WordSearch (right, down and down-right only), WordRace (its 100ms ticker counts tenths; about a third of spawns are the target and a quarter are misspellings of it; every falling word looks the same; words carry the round they fell for and fade when the target changes, and only the target landing breaks the combo — `fallOnce`; the combo bonus stops at 5), ParrotParty (`beatFor` by word length, queued speech; from round 4 the tiles stay dark), BeeBingo (3×3 card of up to 6 words plus look-alike misspellings, free centre; the call is spoken, never shown)
  - Bible*: BibleTriviaEnhanced (a bank of 19 child-level questions with references, 7 a game, questions and options shuffled and read aloud), BibleMemorizer (13 built-in NLT verses, three steps of 2/3/4 blanks in reading order, checked only on Enter/Check; bible-api.com has no NLT, so another `version` is fetched with a 5s fallback to the built-in verse)
  - Hidden* (`HIDDEN_GAMES`, unreachable): BonusGame, ShapeCatcher, PatternMemory, RhythmTap, MemoryGrid, ReactionTest, PatternSequencer, ColorMixer, MathPuzzle, MusicComposer, PhysicsPuzzle, PuzzleSlider
  - Not hub cards: PrizeWheel (daily spin above the grid; pays its own stars exactly once — when the wheel stops, or if it is left mid-spin; key `prizeWheelLastSpin`) and SiblingShowdown (route `/showdown` in `src/App.tsx`, no props, pays its own stars)

## How it talks to other modules
- `src/pages/GameCenter/index.tsx` owns the catalogue: kebab-case ids, `category`, `isNew`, the unlock via `learningFlow.isGameUnlocked(id)`, `HIDDEN_GAMES`, `wordlessGames` and the render switch. `openGame` and the `startGame` route-state shortcut both refuse hidden or locked ids. A refused shortcut (Home's verse button, the Bible tab) shows a `.shortcut-locked` notice saying why. Every lock message on the page — tip, gate banner (with a Start Quiz / Start Review / Learn Words button), Game of the Day, each card — comes from one `blockedBy` answer and `lockedBecause(card)`, so the review step after the quiz is never shown as "every game is unlocked". The header's "← Back to Games" unmounts the game, and that is the child's exit
- While a game is open GameCenter puts `playing-game` on `<body>`, and the bottom navigation slides out of the way (games keep their feedback at the bottom, where the bar sat on top of it on short screens). Above the game is one slim row — "← Back to Games" and the best chip; the hub's `<h1>` is visually hidden because every game draws its own title (the old big title card pushed play ~150px down)
- Medals: `medalFor(plays)` in `GameStats` — 🥉 one finish, 🥈 three, 🥇 seven (`MEDAL_AT`), from the existing `game_play_counts`, so no new storage key. Each card shows its medal, the hub header tallies them ("in 4 of 44 games"), and crossing a threshold adds a `badge` line to the celebration
- The card id is the persistence key for GameStats (bests, plays, favourites, seen, daily challenge), the achievements badge, `FREE_GAMES` and `pickGameOfTheDay`. Renaming an id orphans saved data. Adding a card changes every later day's pick, because the pick is a hash over the sorted ids of the visible `rawGames`. The Game of the Day and the "Next up" suggestion both leave out `HIDDEN_GAMES`, which `openGame` refuses
- Contexts: games only speak through `useAudio().speak` (only the hidden games are silent; every visible game now speaks its word and uses `useCheer`). Only TreasureTrail and SpellTacToe read `useTheme` (for `world.mascot`); only SiblingShowdown uses `useProgress` and `useRewardStore`; PrizeWheel uses `useRewardStore`
- Services: SpellTower, SpellTacToe, WordMachine and DefinitionDetective call `ReviewSchedule.record(wordId, correct)` once per answered word (DragonDuel only on the first go at each word, since a short list cycles), which fires `reviewScheduleUpdated`. WordLock records only its first try (later tries have the lock's letter marks). CrissCross deliberately doesn't: crossing letters help, so a fill is not a clean recall. BeeBingo and HoneyFlight don't either: both pick among offered spellings. No game writes WordMastery, LearningFlow or PointsService. The only event a game fires is SiblingShowdown's `show-confetti`
- Recording is not free: it puts the word into the Leitner schedule, and `ReviewSchedule.dueCount` feeds the review gate that can lock games on a later day. Only record a real recall of that exact word (typing it from sound, its plural, the word from its meaning). Don't record pattern or spot-the-typo rounds
- `shared/sfx` is also imported by ~25 files outside games (SiblingChallenge and its `modes/`, BeeTournament, TrophyRoom, WordHunt, SentenceMaker, buddy, common). ThemeContext calls `setWorldSoundProfile` and `sfx.worldJingle`. SiblingShowdown is unrelated to `src/pages/SiblingChallenge`

## Conventions and gotchas
- CSS: the root class is the kebab name (`.vowel-rescue`); children use a short prefix that must be unique across all games (`vr-`, `os-`, `dt-`; on a clash add a 2: `dd2-`, `wf2-`, `ws2-`). All game CSS ships in one global bundle, so grep a prefix before taking it. Don't copy the old games' bare `.game-*`/`.progress-*` classes
- Rounds: derive them once in `useMemo` on `[providedWords, rounds]`: `shuffle`, filter with `isPlayable(w, 3)`, then slice. A stricter filter than the hub's gate lets an unlocked game open with nothing in it. Use `shared/wordTricks`, not a local shuffle
- Empty state: a friendly message only, with no button. Every `onComplete` call earns at least one star and a daily-challenge play
- Each round: `speak(word, { queue: true })` in an effect keyed on `[index, words]`, plus a "🔊 Hear it again" button. Queued, because the last word's spoken correction ("Nearly! It was cat: c, a, t") runs longer than the pause before the next word, and a plain `speak` cancels it — the letters, the point of the correction, were lost. A word typed from sound alone (and recorded) is asked bee-style, `sayPrompt(word, sentence)`, so a homophone (tail/tale) has its context; SpellTower and DragonDuel do. Reset per-word state in the same `later()` step that moves the index, not in this effect
- Settle each answer once. Use a `useRef` lock, because a state guard loses to a double tap. Then show a verdict flash (`<prefix>-flash ok|bad`), play `sfx.correct` or `sfx.wrong` and speak. Advance after about 1.1–2.4s with timeouts that are tracked and cleared on unmount (`later()` in VowelRescue, OnStage, DoubleTrouble). A stray timer can call `onComplete` after the child has left and pay for a game nobody finished
- Last round: call `onComplete(score + gained)`, because the `score` state hasn't committed yet. `sfx.win()` is optional. GameCenter shows the celebration and unmounts the game, so a game needs no results screen of its own
- Misses: show and say the word ("It was …"), either on the first miss or after 3 (`MISSES_ALLOWED` in VowelRescue). OnStage deliberately ends the word on the first wrong letter (bee rules), then says it. Keep points for partial progress. A wrong answer the child can edit in place must not stay up: CrissCross clears a wrong word after 0.7s (keys wait meanwhile), or every letter of a correction is marked as another try
- Multi-touch: two children share one iPad, so a drag belongs to the `pointerId` that started it; other fingers and taps are ignored until it ends (WordJigsaw, and CaterpillarCrawl's swipes, which also clear on `lostpointercapture`)
- A typed-answer game joins `SPELLING_FILES` in `NoSpellingHelp.test.ts`. An on-screen tap pad is better still (OnStage, VowelRescue, DoubleTrouble)
- No hint may reveal part of the answer while it is being tested. The meaning on request and one blank per letter are fine. The regex bans "show first letter", "Get Hint", "Hint Used" and "revealFirst" anywhere in a file, comments included
- Word scoping: allowed bank methods, sanctioned exceptions (WordChef) and the guard's blind spots are in `src/services/wordBank/CLAUDE.md`. Curated pattern sets (`RHYME_FAMILIES`, HomophoneHero `PUZZLES`, WordFamily `FAMILIES`) teach a pattern and are never a spelling test
- Touch targets are at least 44px both ways (small hands, a shared iPad): icon-only 🔊 buttons, tappable words and letters included. The shared `Button` has `min-height: 44px`. Measure with `getComputedStyle`, not `getBoundingClientRect`: the Browser pane scales an emulated iPad down to fit, so rects read ~60% small
- A game whose instructions are only written says them once at the start (StoryBuilder, TreasureTrail, WordSearch, WordChef, WordWhack, CrissCross), for a child who can't read yet
- Buttons carry `aria-label`s, and tests find elements by those labels and roles. Renaming a label breaks `NewGames.test.tsx` or `InteractiveGames.test.tsx`
- Reduced motion: global.css already stops `animation`. Add a local block for transitions and looping highlights (see `VowelRescue.css`)
- Keep an existing game's score scale steady, because GameStats compares bests across versions. Don't write localStorage from a game (PrizeWheel's spin key is the one exception)
- A real-time game keeps its world in a ref, repaints with a counter and exports its pure step (`crawlOnce`, `plant` in CaterpillarCrawl; `flyOnce`, `makeGate` in HoneyFlight). Lay out the next round in the same step that bumps the index, not in an effect after it, or the first frame shows the old round (CaterpillarCrawl `startRound`, WordJigsaw `makeRound`)
- Dragging uses pointer events with `setPointerCapture`, `touch-action: none` on the thing dragged, and a tap fallback on `onClick`; a click that ends a drag is ignored for 400ms (WordJigsaw). Keyboard play is a `window` keydown listener that skips inputs and reads handlers through a ref

- Whole module: `npx vitest run __tests__/unit/components/games __tests__/unit/components/NoSpellingHelp.test.ts __tests__/unit/pages/GameShortcuts.test.tsx __tests__/unit/pages/GameCatalogue.test.tsx`
- One game: `npx vitest run __tests__/unit/components/games/NewGames.test.tsx -t "Vowel Rescue"`
- `games/NewGames.test.tsx` checks the behaviour of VowelRescue, DoubleTrouble (and `doubleAt`) and OnStage. It renders inside ThemeProvider and AudioProvider, stubs `shared/sfx` with a `vi.hoisted` no-op object, and uses `vi.useFakeTimers` to step past feedback delays. It is the template for a new game's test; add every sfx method the game calls to the stub, because a missing method throws
- `games/InteractiveGames.test.tsx` checks CaterpillarCrawl (including a second finger mid-swipe), SpellTacToe, CrissCross, WordJigsaw and the `letterTraps` helpers, on the same template. It also stubs `ReviewSchedule`, drives the caterpillar through a whole word with a breadth-first autopilot that reads `data-head`/`data-leaf` off the board, pins `Math.random` for the tic-tac-toe bot, and installs a `PointerEvent` shim with `installPointerEvent()` (jsdom has none, so a drag would lose its position and finger)
- `games/MoreGames.test.tsx` checks WordLock (`markTry` with repeated letters, first-try-only recording, keyboard play, the reveal after five), BeeBingo (60 seeded cards per pool: nine squares, a clean line, calls = card words, no real word as a misspelling; a full card to Bingo; the buzz after three wrong taps) and HoneyFlight (`makeGate`, `flyOnce`, a word flown letter by letter, a miss coming round again). It mocks `AudioContext` with a `speak` spy, which is how the tests hear Bingo's calls
- `games/crossword.test.ts` reads every row and column of 60 seeded grids the way a solver would: each run of 2+ letters must be exactly one entry, the grid connected, numbered in reading order, at most 12 squares a side
- `games/plurals.test.ts` checks the plural rules, null-when-unsure, noun gating, decoys and `pickRounds`
- `games/SiblingShowdown.test.tsx` checks that the final answer counts towards the winner. It mocks `wordBank` and `useRewardStore` and uses a real ProgressProvider in a MemoryRouter
- `games/wordScoping.test.ts`: static regex over each `<Game>/index.tsx` (plus learning and pages), WordChef's exact calls pinned; coverage gaps in wordBank CLAUDE.md
- `NoSpellingHelp.test.ts` is static too: `SPELLING_FILES` must render `<SpellingInput`, no raw text `<input>` may appear outside `NOT_SPELLING`, and the no-hint regex applies (LearnMode and most hidden games are skipped)
- `pages/GameShortcuts.test.tsx` checks that the `startGame` shortcut can't open a locked game. It mocks the whole barrel with only BibleTriviaEnhanced and PrizeWheel, so anything GameCenter renders outside the active-game switch must be added to that mock
